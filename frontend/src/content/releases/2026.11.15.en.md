## Pause the audit purge

As a platform administrator you can now hold the nightly purge of the audit log during a security incident: "Pause purge" on the "Platform administration" page, with a reason such as a case or ticket number. Nothing past its retention is deleted until you choose "Resume purge". It takes effect at once. While the purge is paused, platform administrators see a warning on the other admin pages.

## Visit data kept for half a year

Visit data and sign-in moments are now deleted after 180 days instead of 90. Your SSO ID and e-mail address, if you view restricted content, are kept just as long after your last sign-in. During the investigation of a security incident Toon keeps them longer, until the investigation no longer needs them. The "Privacy" page says so too.
