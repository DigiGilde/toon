## Feedback on your site

A site administrator can choose "Allow feedback" on the new "Feedback" tab. The site's previews then get a "Feedback" button. With it a visitor points at a spot or a text on the page, or gives feedback on the whole page, with a screenshot if they want. With "Also on the live site" the button comes on the live site too. Under "Who may give feedback" you choose "Only with an account", "Also with a name they type themselves" or "Also without a name".

The site team reads the feedback on the "Feedback" tab, replies to it and marks it as seen or resolved. You can do that from the terminal too, with `toon feedback`. Someone who gave feedback with their account sees the replies under "My feedback" in the button.
