## Hear about new feedback

Under "Email about new feedback" you choose whether you get an email when new feedback comes in. On your "Profile" you switch it on for every site you work on, on the "Settings" tab of a group for the sites of that group, and on the "Feedback" tab of a site for that site alone. The choice closest to the site wins. The email names the site, the page, the kind of feedback, the time and whether a screenshot came with it, with a link to the feedback in Toon. It never holds the text of the feedback or who sent it, and it comes at most once every ten minutes per site.

An administrator of a site or a group can connect a "Mattermost channel" with an incoming webhook. Every new feedback item then gets a post of its own in that channel, with its text but without the name or email address of who sent it. The text stays in the channel after the feedback is deleted in Toon.
