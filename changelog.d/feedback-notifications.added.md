- Members choose "Email about new feedback" for themselves on their "Profile", on a group's "Settings" tab and on a site's "Feedback" tab. The email names the site, the page, the kind, the time and whether a screenshot came with it, with a link, at most once per ten minutes per site.
- Site and group administrators connect a "Mattermost channel" with an incoming webhook, which gets a post with the text of each new feedback item, on hosts the operator allows in `TOON_FEEDBACK_WEBHOOK_HOSTS`.
- Notification email goes over SMTP from `TOON_SMTP_*` or the `SMTP_*` variables of ZAD's `send-email` service, with `TOON_SMTP_TLS`, `TOON_FEEDBACK_EMAIL_DAILY_MAX`, `TOON_FEEDBACK_EMAIL_WINDOW_MINUTES` and `TOON_FEEDBACK_MATTERMOST_WINDOW_MINUTES`.
- The admin API for it: `GET` and `PUT` on `/me/feedback-email`, `/groups/{group}/feedback-email` and `/sites/{group}/{site}/feedback-email`, and `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE` on `/groups/{group}/feedback-webhook` and `/sites/{group}/{site}/feedback-webhook`.
